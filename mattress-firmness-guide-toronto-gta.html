Why Firmness Matters More Than Price

A mattress that feels fine for five minutes can feel wrong after a full night. Firmness decides whether your spine stays level, your shoulders and hips are cushioned and you wake up rested. In the GTA, where many people rent condos and move often, a wrong choice is an annoying one: returns are not accepted after delivery on our orders, so choose firmness before you order.

The market is also changing for renters. CMHC put apartment vacancy at 3.0% in Toronto in 2025, and asking rents for two-bedroom apartments were about 6% lower in late 2025 than in early 2024 (index 93.7 vs 100). More renters are choosing and moving, and many are buying a mattress without trying it first.

These are general rules of thumb, not medical advice. If you have ongoing back pain, speak to a health professional.
