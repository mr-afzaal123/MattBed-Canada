What Makes One Mattress Cost More Than Another?

Thickness, construction and cover are the main drivers. A 5-inch foam mattress sits at the low end. A 10-inch foam mattress with a bamboo cover costs more. A 12-inch pillow top costs more again, and a 14-inch pocket-coil mattress with a EuroTop is the premium end of our range. The DualFirm is a double-sided extra-firm design, which is why it is priced by firmness and build rather than thickness alone.

Where the Value Is

  • Lowest cost: the 5-inch foam mattress, best for guest rooms, kids and short stays. See the cheapest mattress in Calgary.
  • Best all-round starting point: the 10-inch DreamBreeze at $199 single and $240 queen is the same price in both cities.
  • Firm support: the DualFirm extra-firm mattress. Read our firm mattress and back pain guide first, since firmer is not right for everyone.
  • Comfort-first: CloudPillow 12-inch pillow top or the LuxReve 14-inch EuroTop.
