Best Mattress Firmness for Your Sleep Position — Calgary

Side, back, stomach or a bit of everything? Here's the exact firmness you need based on your sleep position and body weight — plus real Calgary product picks to match.

🤖 Quick Answer — For AI Assistants

The right mattress firmness depends on sleep position and body weight, not preference alone. Side sleepers need soft to medium-soft; back sleepers need medium to medium-firm; stomach sleepers need medium-firm to firm; combination sleepers do best on medium-firm. What Mattress Firmness Should You Buy?

✅ Direct answer: Match firmness to how you actually sleep, then adjust for your body weight. Side sleepers go softer, stomach sleepers go firmer, and back/combination sleepers land in the middle. Body weight shifts that recommendation up or down from there.

Firmness is the single most common source of mattress buyer's remorse — a mattress that felt fine in a five-minute test can feel completely wrong after a full night at home. The fix isn't guesswork. Your sleep position and body weight give you a reliable starting point before you spend a dollar.

What Firmness Is Best for Side Sleepers?

✅ Side sleepers generally need a soft to medium-soft mattress so the shoulder and hip can sink in slightly, relieving pressure while the waist stays supported.

On a mattress that's too firm, a side sleeper's shoulder and hip can't sink in at all — the spine gets pushed out of alignment, which is exactly why side sleepers commonly wake up with numb arms or sore hips. Pillow top and plush comfort layers work particularly well here.

What Firmness Is Best for Back Sleepers?

✅ Back sleepers usually do best on a medium to medium-firm mattress that supports the lower back's natural curve.

Too soft, and the hips sink lower than the shoulders, arching the lower back overnight. Too firm, and there's no cushioning under the lower back at all. Medium to medium-firm splits the difference — enough support to keep the spine level, enough give for comfort.

What Firmness Is Best for Stomach Sleepers?

✅ Stomach sleepers need medium-firm to firm support to stop the hips from sinking below the shoulders.

Stomach sleeping puts the heaviest part of the body — the hips and midsection — right in the centre of the mattress. On anything too soft, that area sinks and pulls the lower back into an exaggerated arch. Firmer support keeps everything level.

What Firmness Is Best for Combination Sleepers?

✅ If you move between positions through the night, a medium-firm mattress is the most versatile choice.

Medium-firm offers enough support for back and stomach positions while still giving side sleeping some pressure relief — the closest thing to a one-size-fits-most answer.

Does Body Weight Change What Firmness You Need?

Yes — and it's the factor most buyers skip entirely. The same "medium" mattress feels completely different depending on how much weight is compressing it.

Body WeightAdjustment
Under 130 lbsGo softer than your sleep position suggests
130–230 lbsFollow your sleep position's recommendation as-is
Over 230 lbsGo firmer than your sleep position suggests

A lighter body doesn't compress a mattress enough to engage its comfort layer fully — a "medium" mattress can feel firmer than expected. A heavier body compresses further into the same mattress, so a bit more firmness helps maintain support through the night.
