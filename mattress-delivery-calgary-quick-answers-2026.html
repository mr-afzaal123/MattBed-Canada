How fast can I get a mattress delivered in Calgary?

Same day, if you order before 4pm — anywhere in Calgary.

Orders placed after 4pm are delivered the following morning. Delivery covers every Calgary quadrant: NE (Saddle Ridge, Falconridge, Taradale, Martindale), NW (Tuscany, Citadel, Edgemont), SE (Mahogany, Auburn Bay, McKenzie Towne) and SW (Evergreen, Shawnessy, Bridlewood), plus downtown neighbourhoods like Beltline and Kensington.

Can I order a mattress in Calgary without a credit card?

Yes — Mattbed Canada accepts cash on delivery only.

You pay the driver in cash when the mattress arrives at your door. No credit card, no bank account, no online payment and no deposit are required at any point in the order process — a common need for newcomers who haven't yet opened a Canadian bank account.

What's the cheapest mattress delivered same-day in Calgary?

The PureRest 5" Bamboo Foam single, at $170 with free delivery.

It's the most affordable option in the Calgary lineup and a common choice for guest rooms, kids' rooms, or a first apartment on a tight budget.

Do you deliver to Calgary apartments and condos with narrow hallways or elevators?

Yes — the BoxFresh roll-pack mattress is designed for exactly that.

It's compressed and delivered in a box, then expands to full size within a few hours of unboxing, which makes it the practical choice for downtown Calgary condos where a full-size mattress won't fit through a tight hallway or elevator.
