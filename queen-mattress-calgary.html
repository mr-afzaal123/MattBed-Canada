Is a Queen the Right Size?

A queen is 60 x 80 inches (152 x 203 cm), 10 inches wider and 5 inches longer than a double. It is the usual choice for couples, and a good one for a single sleeper who wants room to stretch. As a rule of thumb it fits comfortably in a bedroom of about 10 x 10 feet or larger. For a smaller room or a guest bed, a double may be enough.
