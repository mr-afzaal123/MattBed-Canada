Mattress Delivery in an Edmonton Winter: What to Expect and How to Unpack It

Does Same-Day Delivery Still Work When It Is Freezing Outside?

Yes, with one honest caveat: winter can slow a delivery down. Our usual target is about 3 to 5 hours after we confirm your order, seven days a week. Our delivery terms say plainly that heavy traffic, bad weather or a long queue of earlier drop-offs can push a delivery later, and in rare cases to another day. If that happens we message you as soon as we know and agree a new time with you.

Five Things to Do Before the Driver Arrives

  1. Clear the path. Shovel or salt the steps and walkway, and the spot where a vehicle can stop. A mattress is bulky and slippery surfaces slow everyone down.
  2. Tell us about access on WhatsApp. Stairs, an elevator, a narrow hallway or a snowed-in parking area are all useful to know before the order is planned.
  3. Pick your window. If you are only home at certain times, say so and we plan the delivery around it.
  4. Clear a spot indoors. Have the bedroom or a dry entrance ready so the mattress does not sit on a wet floor.
  5. Have cash ready. Payment is cash on delivery after you check the mattress. No card or deposit is needed.

Why a Foam Mattress Feels Firm After the Cold

Foam stiffens when it is cold and softens as it warms. A mattress that has been in a cold vehicle or porch can feel firmer than it will once it reaches room temperature. This is general guidance about foam, not a specific claim about one model. Give the mattress several hours indoors before you judge the firmness, and check it at the door only for what you can see: size, model, thickness, cover and corners.

If your mattress arrives compressed or roll-packed, open it and unpack it following the instructions on the packaging, then leave it flat in a warm room. Expansion can take longer in a cold house. Because returns are not accepted after delivery, check the size, model and condition at the door while the driver is there. Judge the feel after it has warmed up.
