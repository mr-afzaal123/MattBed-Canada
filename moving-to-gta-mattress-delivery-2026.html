What's Actually Happening in the GTA Rental Market

If you're moving in the GTA this year, you're doing so in a genuinely different market than a few years ago. A wave of new condo and purpose-built rental completions has met slower population growth, and the result is a rental market that has shifted toward tenants for the first time in years.

Landlords who previously "just took applications" are now actively competing for reliable tenants with move-in bonuses and rent-free periods. Combined with continued — if slower — immigration to the GTA and delayed homeownership keeping more households renting longer, the practical result is more people signing new leases and moving into new units than the tight-market years of 2021-2023.
