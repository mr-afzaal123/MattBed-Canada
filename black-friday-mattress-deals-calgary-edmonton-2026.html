When Are the Big Mattress Sales in 2026?

The main dates are Black Friday (November 27), Cyber Monday (November 30) and Boxing Day (December 26). Canadian retailers also run promotions around long weekends, including Thanksgiving on October 12. Online-only brands tend to push hardest on Cyber Monday, while in-store retailers lean on Black Friday and Boxing Day clearance.

If you need a bed this month, waiting for November means weeks on a bad mattress. The question is whether waiting actually saves you money, and the next section is how to check.

Seven Checks Before You Trust a “50% Off” Banner

  1. Ignore the percentage, read the final price. Compare the exact size you need, in dollars.
  2. Ask what the “regular” price was last month. A genuine discount can be backed by recent selling prices. Consumer guides suggest a retailer should be able to show you the previous 60–90 days.
  3. Be wary of the same “limited time” sale running for months. A permanent sale is just the price.
  4. Check thickness, materials and cover. A “queen mattress” at a low price can mean 5 inches of foam. Compare like for like.
  5. Add the extras. Delivery fees, old-mattress removal and any restocking fee can change the total.
  6. Ask how payment works. Do you pay before you see it? Is there financing with interest? Is a credit check involved?
  7. Check the return and exchange terms in writing before you pay, whoever the seller is.

Should You Wait for Black Friday?

Wait only if your current mattress is still working and the deal passes the seven checks. If your bed is failing, a guest is arriving, or you have moved into a new place, a week of poor sleep costs more than most promotions save. Mattress sales also come around again: Boxing Day, then Family Day weekend, then spring.
