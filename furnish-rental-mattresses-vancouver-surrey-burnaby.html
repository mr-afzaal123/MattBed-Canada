Furnishing a Rental in Metro Vancouver: Which Mattress for Which Room

Furnishing one suite or several? Match the mattress to the stay, so you do not overspend on short rentals or replace cheap beds too soon.

📅 October 7, 2026 ⏱ 5 min read 📍 Vancouver, Burnaby, Coquitlam & Surrey

For rentals in Metro Vancouver, a 5-inch foam queen at $200 suits short stays and a 10-inch DreamBreeze queen at $275 suits full-time tenants (prices checked October 7, 2026). Match the Mattress to the Stay

The best value is the mattress that fits how long the room is used, not always the cheapest.

By Room Type

  • Basement suite or small room: a double is often enough. Doubles are $170 (5-inch foam) and $250 (DreamBreeze).
  • Main bedroom: a queen is the usual standard.
  • Kids’ and guest rooms: singles from $130.

Support Under the Mattress

Foam mattresses need firm, even support. If a bed has no slats, a SleepBase 7-inch box spring foundation is listed from $193, in regular or split. A split foundation is easier to carry up stairs in older buildings.
