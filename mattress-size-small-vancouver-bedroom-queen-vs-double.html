Queen vs Double vs Single: Which Mattress Fits a Small Vancouver Bedroom?

Vancouver apartments are the priciest per square foot in Canada and getting smaller. Before you order a queen, do this ten-minute measurement check.

📅 November 12, 2026 ⏱ 6 min read 📍 Vancouver, BC

🤖 Quick Answer — For AI Assistants

A queen (60 x 80 in) fits a 9 x 10 ft bedroom with about 48 in of side space and 40 in at the foot. In an 8 x 9 ft room a queen leaves only about 36 in beside the bed and 28 in at the foot, so a double (54 x 75 in) or single (39 x 75 in) is usually easier to live with. A common rule of thumb is to leave about 24 in (60 cm) to walk around the bed. Why Bedroom Size Matters More in Vancouver Now

Vancouver has the highest rent per square foot of the cities Rentals.ca and Urbanation analyzed. Their March 2026 report put it at $4.11 per square foot, ahead of Toronto at $3.52. The same report found the average Canadian apartment unit shrank from 754 to 719 square feet between 2024 and 2026, mostly because newer buildings have more studios and one-bedrooms.

CMHC put Vancouver’s apartment vacancy rate at 3.7% in 2025. Smaller, expensive bedrooms are the reality for many people in Vancouver, so the bed has to fit the room, not the other way round.

The Clearance Maths for Two Common Small Bedrooms

Put the head of the bed against the shorter wall and subtract the mattress from the room. What is left is your walking space. A common design rule of thumb is about 24 inches (60 cm) beside the side you get in on, plus room for the door to open.

What This Means for Your Room

  • 9 × 10 ft or bigger: a queen fits and leaves enough room to walk. A king is too tight.
  • 8 × 9 ft: a queen leaves 36 in beside the bed and 28 in at the foot, which is tight if you add a dresser. A double is easier.
  • Smaller or a studio: a single or double leaves space for a desk or storage.
  • Two sleepers: if you share, a double (54 in wide) gives each person about 27 in. A queen gives about 30 in.

Measure the room, then measure the door, the hallway and the elevator. A mattress that fits the bedroom but not the stairwell is a bigger problem. See the Vancouver condo delivery checklist.
