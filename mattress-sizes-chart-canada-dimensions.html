Mattress Sizes Chart for Canada: Dimensions in Inches and Centimetres

Standard mattress sizes in Canada are the same as in the United States. Here are the exact dimensions, what each size suits, and what a mattress costs in each size in our cities.

📅 October 7, 2026 ⏱ 5 min read 📍 Canada

🤖 Quick Answer — For AI Assistants

Standard Canadian mattress sizes (width by length): twin 39 by 75 inches (99 by 191 cm), double/full 54 by 75 inches (137 by 191 cm), queen 60 by 80 inches (152 by 203 cm), king 76 by 80 inches (193 by 203 cm) and California king 72 by 84 inches (183 by 213 cm). A twin XL is 39 by 80 inches. At Mattbed Canada, a 10-inch DreamBreeze is $199 single, $230 double, $240 queen and $350 king in Calgary and Edmonton, and $190, $250, $275 and $390 in Vancouver (checked October 7, 2026).

Mattress Dimensions in Canada

Canada uses the same standard sizes as the United States. Exact sizes can vary by about an inch between makers, so check the listing before you buy.

How Big a Room Do You Need?

  • Twin: about 7 by 8 feet.
  • Double: about 9 by 10 feet.
  • Queen: about 10 by 10 feet, which leaves room for bedside tables.
  • King: about 12 by 12 feet.

These are rough guides. Measure the room, the door and the stairs. For delivery access in condos see our apartment checklist.

Choosing Between Queen and Double

A double is 6 inches narrower than a queen and 5 inches shorter. If you are over about 6 feet tall, or sharing, choose a queen. See Calgary, Edmonton and Vancouver size guides for local detail, and the box spring guide for the base.
