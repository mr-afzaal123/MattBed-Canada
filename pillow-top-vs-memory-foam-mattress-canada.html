What Actually Makes a Pillow Top Different from Memory Foam?

Before comparing them, it helps to understand what you're actually comparing. These are two completely different construction types — not just two different firmness levels.

What is a pillow top mattress?

A pillow top mattress has an extra layer of soft padding sewn directly onto the top surface of the mattress. This padding is typically made from cotton, foam, polyester, or a combination. The support core underneath is usually high-density foam or innerspring coils. The defining visual feature is a visible gap or seam between the comfort layer and the mattress body — it literally looks like a pillow was placed on top and stitched down.

The result is immediate, luxurious softness when you first lie down — the kind of feel you get in a good hotel. It cushions pressure points quickly and feels plush from the very first night.

What is a memory foam mattress?

A memory foam mattress uses viscoelastic polyurethane foam as its primary comfort layer. This material was originally developed by NASA in the 1970s for aircraft cushioning. It responds to your body heat and weight by slowly conforming to your exact shape — then slowly returning to its original form when you get up.

The result is a deeper, slower-responding hug that gets better over 10–15 minutes of lying down. It distributes your weight evenly, eliminating pressure points at the hips and shoulders. It does not feel the same as a pillow top — it is more enveloping and less immediately bouncy.

💡 Simple way to remember the difference: A pillow top changes how the mattress feels the moment you touch it. Memory foam changes how the mattress supports your body over time. Both feel soft — but they solve different problems.

Who Should Choose a Pillow Top Mattress?

✅ Choose Pillow Top if you...

Pillow top is right for you when:

  • You sleep hot and wake up sweating — pillow tops breathe better than traditional foam
  • You love that hotel-bed feeling — immediately plush and luxurious from night one
  • You sleep on your back or stomach — pillow tops provide surface cushioning without the deep sink of foam
  • You are a heavier sleeper (over 100kg) — foam can sometimes feel too enveloping; pillow tops have a more responsive feel
  • You want a more affordable luxury feel — pillow tops give a premium feel at a lower price than latex or high-end hybrid
  • You don't share the bed — motion isolation is not a concern

Who Should Choose Memory Foam?

✅ Choose Memory Foam if you...

Memory foam is right for you when:

  • You are a side sleeper — memory foam cradles the shoulder and hip, eliminating the pressure points that cause pain for side sleepers
  • You have back, shoulder or hip pain — the contouring of memory foam provides pressure relief that pillow tops can't match
  • You share a bed with a partner who moves at night — memory foam absorbs motion so you don't feel their movements
  • You want a longer-lasting mattress — foam outlasts pillow top padding by 3–5 years on average
  • You live in a cold climate — memory foam retains warmth, which is beneficial in Edmonton winters
  • You want consistent support — foam doesn't shift, dip or bunch like a pillow top comfort layer can

What About Back Pain? Which is Better for Canadians?

Back pain is one of the top three health complaints in Canada. According to research, the most important factor is not foam vs pillow top — it is firmness level. Studies consistently show that medium-firm mattresses reduce back pain better than either very firm or very soft options.

Both pillow top and memory foam can be medium-firm. The key is what's underneath the comfort layer. A pillow top on a high-density foam base gives excellent lumbar support while still cushioning the surface. The Canadian Climate Factor — Heat Matters More Here Than You Think

Traditional memory foam's biggest weakness in Canada is heat retention. Calgary summers regularly reach 32–35°C. Edmonton can hit 30°C+ for weeks. Vancouver summers are warm and humid. In these conditions, a traditional memory foam mattress can make you uncomfortably hot at night.

This is why bamboo-covered foam mattresses are the fastest-growing category in Canada in 2026. Bamboo fabric naturally regulates temperature — cooling when warm, retaining warmth when cold. It gives you the contouring benefits of foam without the heat problem.
