Why Bamboo is the Best Mattress Material for Calgary's Climate

Calgary has one of the most extreme climates in Canada. Summers regularly reach 30–35°C with very low humidity. Winters drop to -20°C or lower. This temperature range is brutal for synthetic mattress fabrics — they feel sticky in summer and cold in winter.

Bamboo-certified fabric solves this problem naturally. Here's why it works so well for Calgary:

  • Temperature regulation — bamboo fibres naturally regulate temperature, staying cool in Calgary's hot dry summers and retaining warmth in winter
  • Moisture-wicking — draws sweat away from your body, keeping you dry all night — important in Calgary's dry climate where dehydration and dry skin are common
  • Hypoallergenic — Calgary's dry air means higher dust and allergen levels. Bamboo resists dust mites and allergens better than synthetic fabrics
  • Breathable — allows airflow through the mattress, preventing the heat build-up common with memory foam in warm rooms
  • Soft and durable — bamboo fabric is smoother than cotton and more durable than polyester
