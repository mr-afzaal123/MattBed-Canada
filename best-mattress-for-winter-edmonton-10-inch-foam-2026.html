Why a 10-Inch Foam Mattress Works Well in Winter

Foam tends to hold body heat, which many people like on a cold night. It is also quiet, it does not shift under you, and 10 inches is the usual starting thickness for a mattress you sleep on every night. That is why we call the 10-inch foam mattress the best all-round starting point in our range. It is not the only good choice: if you sleep hot, prefer a firmer feel or share a bed, message us and we will point you to the right model.

Not sure about firmness? Send us your sleep position and rough weight on WhatsApp. We will suggest a model before you order, and you still check it at your door before you pay.

What the Winter Outlook Says for Edmonton

The seasonal outlook points to a milder winter, not a harsh one. Daily Hive reported in August 2026 that Environment and Climate Change Canada researcher Bill Merryfield expects Alberta temperatures about 2°C above average this winter, with less winter precipitation possible near the Rockies, linked to a strong El Niño. ClimateData.ca (Canadian Centre for Climate Services) likewise says precipitation is most likely below normal across much of Alberta.

That does not mean no snow. A seasonal outlook is an average, and a single snowstorm or cold snap can still slow roads for a day. Ordering your mattress on a calm day is simply the easier option.

Why Order Before a Storm, Not During One

Delivery depends on the road. Our delivery terms say that heavy traffic, bad weather or a long queue of earlier drop-offs can push a delivery later, and in rare cases to another day. We message you as soon as we know and agree a new time. Ordering on a calm day means you are not competing with a storm for the same driver and road.

  3. Clear the entrance and have a dry spot ready indoors.
  4. Check the mattress at the door (size, model, thickness, cover), then pay cash. Returns are not accepted after delivery.
