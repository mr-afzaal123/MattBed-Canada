Best Mattress in Vancouver: Casper, Sleep Country, Endy & More Compared

We checked the real delivery times and payment methods behind Vancouver's most-searched mattress options. Here's what we found — and where the gaps actually are.

What We Actually Checked

✅ Direct answer: If speed and payment flexibility matter to you, same-day delivery and cash-on-delivery are the two things worth checking before you buy — and almost every major option in Vancouver is missing at least one.

When you search "mattress in Vancouver," you'll mostly find national brands and direct-to-consumer companies with big marketing budgets. What doesn't always show up clearly is how fast they actually deliver, and whether you need a credit card to buy from them. We looked into both, using each company's own published delivery and payment policies.

Casper: Fast Marketing, Slower Delivery

Casper's standard shipping takes 2 to 9 business days to Canadian addresses via UPS. If you want the mattress actually brought into your room and set up — not just left at your door in a box — that's a separate "In-Home Delivery and Setup" service costing an extra $99, and appointments are typically booked 2 to 4 weeks out. There's no cash-on-delivery option; payment is collected online at checkout.

Sleep Country: The Local Showroom, National Shipping Times

Despite having physical stores across Vancouver, Sleep Country's actual delivery timeline is 2 to 10 business days for standard delivery. Their "Green Glove" service — which includes room delivery, setup, and old mattress removal — costs an additional $45 to $150 depending on the source. Payment is by credit card, debit, or Affirm financing; prepaid cards are explicitly not accepted.

Endy: The Fastest of the Ship-to-You Brands

Endy is genuinely quicker than Casper or Sleep Country for Metro Vancouver — 2 to 3 business days for mattresses. That's still not same-day, and Endy requires payment by credit card, PayPal, Apple Pay, or financing through Affirm at checkout.

King of Mattresses: Same-Day, But Card Only

Of everyone on this list, King of Mattresses comes closest to Mattbed Canada's model — they're a genuine Vancouver local business offering same-day delivery. The difference is payment: their listed payment options are credit cards and NFC mobile payments, with no cash-on-delivery option available.

IKEA and Wholesale Furniture Brokers: Not Delivery-First

IKEA's mattress model is built around either visiting the store yourself or booking a separate paid delivery slot — it's a warehouse retail model, not a dedicated same-day delivery service. Wholesale Furniture Brokers (gowfb.ca) ships mattresses via standard courier, with delivery timelines closer to national online retailers than to a local same-day service.

💬 The pattern across all six: you either wait several business days, or you pay extra for faster room delivery — and every one of them requires a credit card, debit card, or online payment method. None offer genuine same-day delivery with cash paid at your door.
