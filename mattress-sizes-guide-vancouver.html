Should You Buy a Queen or King Mattress in Vancouver?

This is the most common size decision Vancouver buyers face. A queen mattress fits comfortably in most Vancouver bedrooms without crowding the room, and it remains the standard choice for couples across Canada. A king is worth the extra footprint if any of the following apply to you:

  • Your bedroom is 150 square feet or larger
  • One partner is a restless sleeper
  • Pets regularly sleep on the bed
  • One partner runs noticeably warmer or colder than the other and needs separation

If none of those apply, a queen is very likely the right purchase — and it's much easier to move through condo elevators and narrow apartment hallways than a king.

Single/Twin vs Double/Full: What's the Difference?

These two sizes get confused often. A single/twin mattress measures 38" x 75" and is built for one sleeper — common in kids' bedrooms, guest rooms, or a first apartment. A double/full mattress measures 54" x 75", which is 16 inches wider than a single. That extra width makes a double comfortable for one adult who wants to stretch out, though it's snug for two adults sharing long-term.

Measure Before You Buy

Vancouver living often means condos and apartments with tight hallways, narrow doorways and freight elevators with strict booking windows. Before you purchase a queen or king mattress, measure your bedroom doorway, hallway width, and check your building's elevator or stairwell access — mattresses don't bend, so tight spaces can turn delivery day into a problem.
