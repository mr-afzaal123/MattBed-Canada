What Does a Nightstand with Wireless Charging Actually Do?

A nightstand with wireless charging has a built-in charging coil under its top surface, usually a glass or laminate panel. Setting a Qi-compatible phone flat on that surface charges it with no cable, replacing the tangle of cords that usually collect next to a bed.

The Digital Lightning Nightstand puts this charging coil under a black tempered-glass top, so the nightstand looks like ordinary furniture until a phone is placed on it. It plugs into a standard wall outlet through a single power connector on the side — the same connection also powers the light, speaker and USB ports.

Regular Nightstand vs. Smart Nightstand with Charging Station: What's the Difference?

A regular nightstand is just storage — drawers or shelves with no electronics. A smart nightstand with a charging station adds wireless charging, USB ports, and often lighting or a speaker, so the same surface that holds a lamp also powers and entertains the devices sitting on it.

How Do I Use the Wireless Charging and Speaker?

Plug the nightstand into a wall outlet once, using the built-in power connector on the side. From there, place a phone on the glass top to charge wirelessly, touch the surface to control the light, and pair Bluetooth from the phone's settings to use the speaker.

  1. Plug in the power connector on the side of the nightstand to a standard wall outlet — this single connection powers the charger, light, speaker and USB ports.
  2. Place your phone on the glass top, centered over the charging coil, to begin wireless charging.
  3. Touch the surface to turn the ambient light on or off.
  4. Pair Bluetooth from your phone's settings, selecting the nightstand's speaker — it reconnects automatically after the first pairing.

💡 Good to know: most phone cases under 5mm thick do not block wireless charging. Very thick or metal-backed cases can interfere with the signal and may need to come off.
