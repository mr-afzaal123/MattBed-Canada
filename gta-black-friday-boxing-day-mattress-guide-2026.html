The Two Biggest Mattress Sale Events in Canada

Black Friday (November 27, 2026) and Boxing Day (December 26) are consistently the two largest mattress sale periods in the Canadian retail calendar, with advertised discounts typically ranging from 25 to 50 percent. Boxing Day in particular tends to be strong for floor models and discontinued styles, as retailers clear inventory before the new year.

⚠️ A well-documented pattern in the mattress industry: some retailers raise their "regular" price in October specifically so the November Black Friday markdown looks larger than it actually is. The advertised percentage off isn't always calculated from a genuine baseline price.

How to Spot a Real Discount

The only reliable way to check whether a Black Friday or Boxing Day mattress deal is genuine is to compare the retailer's price in October against their "sale" price in November or December. If you didn't track the October price yourself, look for cached versions of the product page, or simply ask the retailer directly what the price was a month earlier.

The alternative: consistent pricing year-round

A different approach some retailers take — including Mattbed Canada — is keeping prices stable throughout the year rather than inflating and discounting on a seasonal cycle. This means there's no risk of paying an artificially high October price to "save" less in November, because the price simply doesn't move with the calendar.
