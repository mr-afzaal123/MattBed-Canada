Why Calgary Movers Have More Room to Choose in 2026

Calgary apartment prices are lower than a year ago. Liv.rent reported that the average unfurnished one-bedroom apartment in Calgary cost $1,467 a month in June 2026, down 4.55% from June 2025. CMHC put Calgary’s apartment vacancy rate at 5.0% in 2025, up from the very tight levels of 2022 and 2023.

That means more apartments to choose from and more room to negotiate. It also means more people moving in and out, and a bedroom to furnish at short notice. If you are moving into a new place this month, the bedroom is the one room you need working on day one.

The Order to Buy Things in a New Calgary Bedroom

  1. Mattress first. Sleep is the one thing you cannot delay. A mattress on the floor is a workable first night if the frame has not arrived.
  2. Bed frame second. A frame lifts the mattress off the floor and helps with airflow. Our Calgary and Edmonton bed frames start from $200, and the price depends on the style. See same-day bed frames.
  3. Check whether you need a box spring. Many modern frames do not need one. Read Do I need a box spring? before you pay for one.
  4. Bedding and a pillow last. These are cheap to add once the bed itself is in place.

Furnished vs Unfurnished: Is It Worth Buying Your Own?

Liv.rent put the average furnished one-bedroom in Calgary at $1,610 in June 2026, about $143 a month more than unfurnished. Over 12 months that gap is more than the cost of a queen mattress and a frame together, and the bed is yours to keep when you move. If your apartment is unfurnished, buying a bed is usually the cheaper route.
