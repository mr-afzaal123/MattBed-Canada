Furnishing a Rental in Calgary or Edmonton: Which Mattress for Which Room

Furnishing one room or six? Match the mattress to how long each tenant or guest will stay, so you do not overspend on short stays or replace cheap beds too soon.

📅 October 7, 2026 ⏱ 5 min read 📍 Calgary & Edmonton

For rentals in Calgary and Edmonton, use a 5-inch foam mattress (from $110 Calgary, $130 Edmonton) for short stays and a 10-inch DreamBreeze (single $199, double $230, queen $240) for full-time tenants. Single Rooms by Size

  • Single bedroom or basement suite: a single or double is often enough, and doubles start at $150 (Calgary) and $170 (Edmonton) in 5-inch foam.
  • Master bedroom: a queen is the usual standard. Check the size guide against the room.
  • Kids’ rooms and guest rooms: singles from $110 (Calgary) and $130 (Edmonton).

Support, Frames and Boxes

Foam mattresses need firm, even support. If a bed has no slats, add a foundation ($161 single, $205 double, $248 queen). Read do you need a box spring for the quick test. For frames, message us for current styles on WhatsApp.
