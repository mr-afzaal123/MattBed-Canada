What You Give Up at the Lowest Prices

Thickness is the main trade-off. A 5-inch foam mattress is the cheapest way to sleep off the floor, and it is a reasonable choice for guests, kids and short-term rentals. If it will be your nightly bed for years, a thicker mattress generally gives more cushioning and tends to be the better buy, and the 10-inch DreamBreeze is the step up that stays under $300 in a queen.

Back pain is the exception to “buy the firmest.” Firmness is personal, so read our firm mattress and back pain guide and speak to a health professional about ongoing pain.
