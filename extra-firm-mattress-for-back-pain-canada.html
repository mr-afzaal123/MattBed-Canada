Does an Extra Firm Mattress Help Back Pain?

It depends on the person, and firmer is not automatically better. The best-known trial, published in The Lancet in 2003, followed 313 adults with chronic non-specific low-back pain. After 90 days, those given a medium-firm mattress did better on pain in bed and on pain-related disability than those given a firm one. The trial did not test extra firm, so it can't tell us how extra firm compares.

🔎 Honest summary: choose extra firm if you like a hard surface and dislike sinking in. If you wake up with aches at your shoulders or hips, a mattress with a little more give may feel better. Read our full look at the research in Firm vs Medium-Firm Mattress for Back Pain.

Source: Kovacs FM et al., The Lancet 2003;362:1599–1604 — PubMed abstract.

"Medicated" and "Orthopedic" Mattress: What the Words Mean

Many shoppers search for a "medicated mattress" or an "orthopedic mattress". Neither is a certified medical category. There is no test a mattress has to pass to be sold under those names, so they usually just describe a firmer, more supportive feel. We describe the DualFirm by what it is: an extra firm, double-sided, high-density foam mattress. We do not claim that any mattress treats, cures or relieves a medical condition.
