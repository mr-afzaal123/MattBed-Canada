Why Bedframe Choice Matters More in a Small Apartment

In a small apartment or basement suite — which describes most housing for newcomers in Calgary NE, Edmonton's Mill Woods and Burnaby — the bed takes up 60 to 70 percent of the bedroom floor space. This means the bedframe style you choose affects not just how the room looks but how functional your entire home is.

The right bedframe style in a small room does three things:

  • Creates storage — a storage bedframe with drawers replaces a separate dresser, freeing up wall space
  • Makes the room look larger — the right height and style creates an illusion of more space
  • Lasts longer — a quality frame protects your mattress and extends its lifespan by years

💡 Canadian apartments average 650 square feet — and bedrooms are typically 100 to 130 square feet. Every centimetre matters. Choose your bedframe style the same way you choose your mattress — with purpose.

Which Style is Best for Your Situation?

If storage is your biggest problem — Storage Bedframe

Most small apartments in Canada have little to no closet space. If you arrived in Canada recently and are setting up your first home, a storage bedframe with built-in drawers is one of the smartest choices — it gives you several deep drawers for clothes, bedding and household items without taking any extra floor space. You don't need to buy a separate dresser.

If style matters — Velvet Bedframe

The velvet upholstered bedframe is a consistently popular request across Calgary and Edmonton. It typically comes in neutral colours that work in any apartment, and the padded headboard makes sitting up in bed comfortable for reading or working from bed. It makes a plain apartment bedroom look finished and intentional.

If you want minimal and modern — Black Leather

The black leather bedframe has a slim, low-profile design that makes small rooms look larger. It's easy to clean — just wipe it down. A popular request among professionals and students in Vancouver and Calgary who want a clean, modern look.

If budget is your main concern — Platform Bedframe

A simple platform bedframe is typically our most affordable style. Completely functional, durable and noise-free. If you're setting up a guest room or a child's room and don't need anything elaborate, this is usually the right call.

5 Things to Consider When Choosing a Bedframe for a Small Room

1

Measure before you order

A queen mattress is 60 × 80 inches (152 × 203 cm). Add 5 to 10 cm on each side for the bedframe. Make sure you have at least 60 cm walkway on each side of the bed and at the foot.

2

Do you need a box spring?

Most bedframes have built-in slats, so your mattress sits directly on the frame — no box spring needed. If you're using our budget 5 Inches Foam Medium Firm mattress and want extra height, a SleepBase 7" Box Spring is available separately.

3

Height matters for storage

A higher bedframe gives more under-bed clearance for storage boxes. A lower bedframe makes the room look more spacious. A storage bedframe solves this dilemma — it stores inside the frame, not underneath.

4

Consider your elevator

If you live in an apartment building, the bedframe has to fit in your elevator. Let us know your floor and unit number when you message us so we can plan delivery accordingly.

5

Ask about mattress and bedframe together

If you're ordering both, mention it on WhatsApp — we'll do our best to coordinate delivery together so you get a complete bedroom setup in as few trips as possible.
