5 Things to Consider When Choosing a Bedframe for a Small Room

1

Measure before you order

A queen mattress is 60 × 80 inches (152 × 203 cm). Add 5 to 10 cm on each side for the bedframe. Make sure you have at least 60 cm walkway on each side of the bed and at the foot.

2

Do you need a box spring?

No. All Mattbed Canada bedframes have built-in slats. You place your mattress directly on the slats. No box spring needed — this saves money and keeps the bed lower profile.

3

Height matters for storage

A higher bedframe gives more under-bed clearance for storage boxes. A lower bedframe makes the room look more spacious. The storage bedframe solves this dilemma — it stores inside the frame, not underneath.

4

Consider your elevator

If you live in an apartment building, the bedframe has to fit in your elevator. All Mattbed Canada bedframes come in multiple pieces and are carried up in parts by our delivery team. Add your floor and unit number in the order notes.

5

Order mattress and bedframe together

Mattbed Canada delivers both in one trip — same day, no extra charge. You get a complete bedroom setup today without multiple deliveries or extra fees.
