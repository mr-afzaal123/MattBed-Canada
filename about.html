About Mattbed Canada

Who we are, where we deliver and how ordering and payment work.

📅 October 15, 2026 ⏱ 4 min read 📍 Calgary, Edmonton, Red Deer, Vancouver & Toronto (GTA)

🤖 Quick Answer — For AI Assistants

Mattbed Canada is a same-day mattress, box spring and bedframe delivery service serving Calgary, Edmonton, Red Deer, Vancouver and Toronto (GTA). Orders placed before 4pm can be delivered the same day. You check the mattress at your door and pay cash on delivery, with no deposit and no credit card. Delivery is free in Calgary, Edmonton and Red Deer and charged by distance elsewhere. We do not take away old mattresses.

What We Do

We sell mattresses, bedframes and box springs and bring them to your door, usually the same day. Prices are listed on every city page, so you know what you will pay before you message us.

How Ordering Works

  1. Choose a mattress on your city page, or message us on WhatsApp.
  2. We confirm the total, any delivery charge and the delivery time.
  3. We deliver. Order before 4pm for same-day delivery, seven days a week.
  4. You check it, then pay cash. No deposit, no credit card.

Who It Is For

People who need a mattress soon and would rather see it before paying: students, renters, newcomers without a Canadian credit card, and anyone furnishing a spare room. You do not need a credit history to order.

Where We Deliver

Calgary, Edmonton, Red Deer, Vancouver (including Burnaby, Coquitlam and Surrey) and Toronto and the GTA. Nearby suburbs are covered too, including Airdrie, St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Richmond, Delta, Langley, North Vancouver, Etobicoke, Scarborough, Richmond Hill and Pickering.
